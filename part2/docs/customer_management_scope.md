# Customer-management assumption

For the analytical case study, assume the customer-success team manages every
purchasing account. Reseller behavior and partner ownership are outside scope.
This is a declared simplification, not a statement about PANW's actual sales model.

Sales-route fields may remain in original synthetic source files for provenance.
They are excluded from canonical modeling tables, every model feature set,
policy rankings and review-eligibility filters. The generator's route labels were
metadata, not a driver of cancellation outcomes. No source transactions or
outcomes need to be regenerated for this analytical simplification.

Every ranking policy uses the same review population after existing contract,
date and outcome-availability checks. Customer management is assumed rather than
inferred from sales-route metadata. Prior contact history, measurement quality,
active escalation and urgent renewal handling still require manual review before
outreach. These checks concern readiness to contact customers.
